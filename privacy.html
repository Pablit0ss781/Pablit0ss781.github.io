Privacy policy - Jarvis Perso

Last updated: November 2026

Jarvis Perso is a personal application used only by its owner, on the owner's own computer. It is not offered to other users.

Data accessed

With the owner's consent, the application accesses the owner's own Google data: Gmail messages (read-only), Google Calendar events (read, and creation or deletion of events it created itself) and Google Tasks (read, and creation of tasks).

How the data is used

The data is used only to produce the owner's daily briefing and to add tasks and calendar events for the owner. It is processed locally on the owner's computer by a local language model. It is not sold, shared, transferred to third parties, or used for advertising.

Storage

Authorization tokens and generated briefings are stored only on the owner's computer. The owner can revoke access at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions.

Compliance

The application's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Politique de confidentialité (français)

Jarvis Perso est une application personnelle, utilisée uniquement par son propriétaire sur son propre ordinateur. Elle accède, avec son consentement, à ses propres données Google (Gmail en lecture, Agenda, Tasks) pour préparer son briefing quotidien et ajouter des tâches et événements. Les données sont traitées localement, ne sont ni vendues, ni partagées, ni utilisées pour de la publicité. L'accès peut être révoqué à tout moment sur myaccount.google.com/permissions.
