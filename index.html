Jarvis Perso

Jarvis Perso is a private, single-user assistant that runs on its owner's own computer. It builds a daily briefing from the owner's own Gmail, Google Calendar and Google Tasks, reads it aloud, and can add tasks and calendar events for the owner.

It is not distributed to the public and has exactly one user: its owner.
